🎬 Teleprompter

A scrolling prompter for podcast ad reads — a booth control page and an in-room display, synced live over Firebase. Paste straight from the Google Doc and your colors & highlights come along.

🎛️ Control
Open in the booth (or on a phone/laptop). Load copy, set speed, play/pause.
📺 Display
Fullscreen this on the in-room monitor. Use Copy Display URL on the control page for the matching session link.
🔒 Never on the livestream: the Display is a plain browser window, not an OBS source — so there's no scene and no capture for the audience to ever see.
